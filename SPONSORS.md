# Sponsor Us

Our research focuses on generative art and design intelligence by blending algorithmic design, vector data visualization, and procedural drafting, implemented natively in PostScript and $\LaTeX$. Our work bridges traditional mathematical programming with modern creative coding, enabling machines to interpret logic-driven scripts into masterpieces for print and screen.

For industrial applications, we offer advanced technology solutions to manufacturing, energy, environment,
healthcare, agriculture, and education sectors. Please generously support our open source work.
